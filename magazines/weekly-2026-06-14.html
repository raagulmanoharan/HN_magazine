CRAFT + RENDERING MORNING EDITION — 03 / 10 — SUN JUN 14 2026
Every
Frame
Perfect

"60fps is not enough if the frames aren't arriving when they should."

— tonsky.me

Tonsky digs into why some UIs feel buttery smooth and others feel wrong even at 60fps — the answer is frame timing, not just frame rate. Essential reading for anyone who has ever argued about perceived latency with an engineer who insists 60fps is 60fps.

Why it matters

Frame rate is a count.
Frame timing is a rhythm.

A dropped frame, a late frame, a frame that arrives 2ms early — each one registers in the human visual system as a stutter, even if your profiler shows a perfect average.

Consistency beats speed.
