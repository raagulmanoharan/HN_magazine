PROTECT- PRIVACY in an AI Era
Broken Mechanisms
Cookie consent dialogs
GDPR opt-out toggles
Individual data control

Schneier surfaces Daniel Solove's argument that individual data-control frameworks — consent dialogs, GDPR toggles — are fundamentally broken in the AI era. Accountability for downstream harm is the only lever that actually works. Relevant framing for anyone designing consent UX.

Consent is theatre. When AI can infer, combine, and act on data at scale, asking users to click "I agree" doesn't protect them. It protects the company. Real privacy requires holding organizations liable for the harms they cause — not shifting the burden onto individuals.
