On implicit decisions & undocumented rationale

Every design system contains decisions that exist only in someone's head. The undocumented rationale embedded in every component — AI tools are now forced to guess at it, or ignore it entirely. If you can't articulate it, the model will make something up.

  • Why we never use shadows below 4px
  • The icon grid exception for 20px targets
  • Why that border-radius is 6px not 8px
  • The motion rule we agreed on in a Slack thread
  • The colour that's wrong in Figma but right in prod

The model doesn't know what you meant.
It only knows what you wrote.
